## How to support

1. Share Precogly with your security team. They'll appreciate having a free, open source threat modeling platform 🙂.
2. Raise an issue
3. Make a PR (we have lots of newbie friendly issues!)

## A small brief about your project

OWASP Precogly is an open-source threat modeling platform that helps security teams collaboratively create, analyze, and maintain threat models. It combines reusable threat libraries, risk analysis, and AI assistance to make threat modeling faster, more consistent, and accessible to developers and security engineers alike.
