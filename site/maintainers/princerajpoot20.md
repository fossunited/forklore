## How to support

You can support AsyncAPI (part of Linux Foundation) by contributing to repositories, helping with community discussions, and supporting the initiative financially through Open Collective. Community support in the form of mentorship, onboarding help, and contributor guidance also plays an important role in sustaining the ecosystem.

More details on sponsorship: https://opencollective.com/asyncapi#category-CONTRIBUTE

## A small brief about your project

AsyncAPI — part of the Linux Foundation — is an open source initiative focused on building specifications, tooling, and documentation for event-driven architectures. It helps developers design, document, and manage asynchronous APIs and event-driven systems consistently across different technologies and platforms.

## One FOSS maintainer lesson for your younger self

Sustainable open source is less about doing everything yourself and more about building processes, trust, and contributor ownership early.
