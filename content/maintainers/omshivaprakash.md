## How to support

People can get involved in a few ways: contribute code by opening pull requests on any of our GitHub repos (fonts, digitization tools, dictionaries, transliteration tools, etc.); help grow our book and periodical archives by submitting scans, bibliographic data, or missing entries (e.g. via JSON/data files for Pustaka Sanchaya, Patrika Sanchaya, or author-specific collections); contribute to font development and quality assurance by testing fonts, reporting glyph or rendering issues, or designing new Kannada typefaces; help with translation, transliteration, or proofreading of digitized texts; or adopt and help maintain one of our existing projects long-term. Beyond code and content, people can also support us by reaching out directly at [info@sanchaya.org](mailto:info@sanchaya.org), or contribute financially through our nonprofit partner Sanchi Foundation, which funds server costs, digitization work, and new development.

## A small brief about your project

Kannada Language Technology Research & Study Forum
‘Sanchaya’ was born with an intent to make an impact in the area of Kannada & Technology. ‘Sanchaya’ is exploring the possibilities of using technology in Linguistics and Literary research. Tools designed and developed by ‘Sanchaya’ is available for the larger community through Internet and Mobile platforms. ‘Vachana Sanchaya’ is a platform built to enable research on Vachana Literature which is already available for public. We have dreams to build many more such initiatives. Projects binding both Literary & Technology Knowledge through our platforms are based on the inspiration of creating atmosphere around Free Information & Free Knowledge through Free Software. Sanchaya is a not for profit platform working around building the technological capacity around Kannada Literary research.

## One FOSS maintainer lesson for your younger self

Start smaller than you think you need to. It's easier to expand a focused project than to maintain. It's a marathon, not a sprint - pace yourself, because the work of preserving a language's literature never really 'finishes'.

## Why do you do it? Why do you bother maintaining a FOSS project?

Because it's bigger than me - every book we save, every font we revive, every tool we build for preserving our language outlives any single maintainer. That's worth the years of unpaid weekends.
