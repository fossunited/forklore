## How to support

Star the repos, try them and file issues. Good first issues: https://github.com/Sprout-DevLabs/sprout/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22

## A small brief about your project

Sprout is a map of your codebase for you and your AI agent: where to start reading, what a change could break, and which tests to run. Flow is a shared study room where the sun is the timer: it sets while you focus and rises on your break, for everyone in the room at once.

## One FOSS maintainer lesson for your younger self

Write the README before the code. If you can't explain the thing in one line, nobody is going to install it.

## Why do you do it? Why do you bother maintaining a FOSS project?

Building in the open keeps me honest. Every shortcut is visible, every bug is public, and anyone can come along and make it better than I could alone.

## Which file in your project would you most like to set on fire?

## What's your open-source villain origin story?

Opening a big repo, reading a README that said only `npm start`, and spending an afternoon grepping to find where anything lived. Then watching my AI agent do the exact same thing. Sprout is the revenge.
