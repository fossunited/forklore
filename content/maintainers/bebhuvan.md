## How to support

I could really use help in two areas:
1. **Proofreading** — helping check digitized pages against the original archival scans (at https://proof.akshara.ink) so the editions get cleaner over time.
2. **Technical advice** — guidance on optimizing vision-language models (VLMs) and LLMs for historical text extraction, layout formatting, and running these digitization pipelines reliably at scale.

## A small brief about your project

Project Akshara (along with its sister project, Paper Lanterns) is a solo effort to digitize and preserve public-domain historical books and collections of letters—especially India's forgotten intellectual and economic heritage—from archival scans into clean, modern formats (web, EPUB, Markdown, and structured datasets) that anyone can read, download, and reuse for free.

## Why do you do it? Why do you bother maintaining a FOSS project?

I'm a reader, and I've always been a reader. Pretty much every good thing that has happened to me in life is downstream of either the written word or the spoken word. More importantly, I've always had a deep desire to pay my debts. I am where I am today because I've drawn from the common well of knowledge and haven't put much back into it. The commons can only thrive when there is a balance between giving and taking — and this is my small attempt to assuage my own guilt of being more of a taker than a giver.
