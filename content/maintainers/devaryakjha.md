## A small brief about your project

I maintain Oore CI, Tagflow, and gpuicn. They cover mobile CI and distribution, rich content in Flutter, and open-code UI components for GPUI.

## One FOSS maintainer lesson for your younger self

Don't try to perfect everything before you share it. No matter how carefully you work, you will miss something. Let people point out the flaws, fix them, and keep improving.
