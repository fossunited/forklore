## A small brief about your project

Termux is a terminal emulator for Android. We provide pre-compiled software for Android so that you can use your mobile phone as a full fledged Linux environment. In minutes you can get a development environment setup with most major programming languages.

## One FOSS maintainer lesson for your younger self

## Why do you do it? Why do you bother maintaining a FOSS project?

It all started with a Discord bot being not able to kept up to date with discord.js due to out of date Node.js. With help from other folks I managed to update Node.js and eventually took maintainership of it, and just kept on doing it for fun.
